    Table Talk

    How to play

    Goal: be the first to race all 4 of your marbles around the board and into your Home lane.

    On your turn

    1. Roll the die (tap it, hit the Roll button, or press Space).
    2. Roll a 1 or 6 to bring a marble out of Base. A 6 always rolls again.
    3. Tap a glowing marble to move it clockwise by your roll. If it has more than one option, tap where it should land.

    🚀 Jump Start (house rule)

    New marbles pop out 2 spaces ahead of the classic start, on the corner of your lane, so they head straight up toward the middle. Roll a 6 to get out, roll a 5 next, and you land right on a shortcut pad. The host can switch this off in the lobby for the classic start.

    💥 Bumping

    • Land exactly on another player's marble and it flies back to their Base.
    • You can hop over other players, but never over or onto your own marbles.
    • Marbles in Base and Home are safe. Everywhere else is fair game, even the shortcut pads and the center.

    ⚡ Shortcut pads

    • Land exactly on a gold pad. On a later turn that marble may zip clockwise from pad to pad, then keep going down the track with whatever is left of the roll.
    • Jump off at the pad nearest your Home (it's on your right as you face the middle).

    🌀 The whirlpool (center)

    • Land in the center by exact count: it's one step past any pad (from a pad, only a 1 gets you in).
    • The only way out is rolling a 1, and then you can jump to any pad.

    🏠 Getting home

    • After a full lap a marble turns into its Home lane just before its start. You need an exact roll, and you can't pass your own marbles.
    • If you have a legal move you must take one. No move? Your turn passes (unless you rolled a 6).

    Tip: drag the board to spin it, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap 🎥 for a flat top-down view.
